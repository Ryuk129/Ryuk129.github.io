How to Use AI to Improve Your CV

Quick answer: paste your existing CV and a job advert into an AI tool, ask it to tighten and tailor your wording, then check every line yourself before sending.

What you'll need

  • Your current CV (even a rough one)
  • The job advert you're applying for
  • Any free AI chat tool

Step 1: Choose a tool

Any free AI chat assistant will do. Don't pay for anything yet.

Step 2: Give it your real experience

Paste in your CV. Only use facts that are true; the tool can't know what you actually did.

Step 3: Ask for structure

Try: "Rewrite my work experience as short bullet points starting with action verbs. Don't invent anything."

Step 4: Check every claim and edit it yourself

AI can exaggerate or add details you never gave it. Remove anything you couldn't back up in an interview.

Step 5: Tailor it to the job

Paste the job advert and ask: "Which of my skills match this role? Suggest changes to my summary, without adding anything false."

Example

Before: "Responsible for helping customers in the shop."
After: "Served 60+ customers daily, resolving queries and processing payments accurately."

Only use numbers that are true, so swap in your own.

Common mistakes

  • Sending the AI's first draft unchecked
  • Using the same CV for every application
  • Letting the tool invent skills or job titles

FAQ

Will employers know I used AI? Generic, buzzword-heavy wording is the giveaway. Edit in your own voice.

Is it free? Yes, free tools are enough for this.
